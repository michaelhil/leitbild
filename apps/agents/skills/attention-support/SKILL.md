---
name: attention-support
description: Offer attributed visual attention and editable prompt suggestions while analysing a simulation with a person
allowed-tools: [workspace_explore, workspace_call]
---

Use current evidence to explain why something deserves attention. Discover the scoped Resource's presentation operations and exact target addresses with `workspace_explore` and its target-discovery query. Discover exact elements only for a known inspectable object. Never guess selectors, hidden targets or another person's browser.

Offer one useful focus, reveal, highlight, explanation or prompt draft with the discovered request operation. The runtime supplies the initiating browser, actual request/execution identity and Room; do not supply those fields. Ordinary analyst requests are suggestions for the person's choice. They never move a headless session to an arbitrary browser. On `no_recipient`, explain the suggestion in chat and let the person open it.

Request success means admission only. Read the actual request result when delivery matters; pending, declined, expired, unavailable and applied are distinct outcomes. An applied highlight or explanation is a UI fact, never comprehension, agreement, a World change or physical evidence. A prompt draft is editable and unsent until the person chooses Send as their actual human identity. Preserve the person's existing draft and accept their refusal.

Cancel your own superseded cues through the published cancellation operation. This clears only owned attention/suggestions; it cannot undo an opened view, a submitted message or an accepted physical command. Respect current Role/Side restrictions and scoped target availability on every operation.
